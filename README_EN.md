## 🌟 Key Features

### ⚔️ 1v1 Arena Tournament
- **Just Guard (Parrying)**: Perfectly timed blocks negate damage and trigger a time-distortion effect.
- **Directional Hit Reaction**: Realistic hit montages based on impact direction for maximum impact.
- **Guard Aim Offset**: Characters maintain focus on the enemy even while in a defensive stance.

### 🧠 Self-Learning AI
- **Adaptive Difficulty**: `ATSEnemyController` analyzes player attack frequency and defensive patterns in real-time to evolve its responses.

### 🎲 Roguelike Growth & Hub
- **Infinite Potential**: Select random traits after each round and experiment with different weapon types at the Hub's weapon station.
