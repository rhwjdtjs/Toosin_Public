# TOOSIN update history and development plan

[한국어](ROADMAP.md) · [Game overview](README_EN.md)

Updated October 1, 2026.

TOOSIN launched on August 13, 2026 (Korea time) and is available on Steam and STOVE. This document records public updates and current development.

## Preparing Version 1.4

Codex and Claude assist work on the hub and menus, appearance and class selection, combat animations, AI, saving and localization. Development builds, automated checks and runtime captures are compared after implementation. The final change list will be prepared after packaging and remaining play checks.

The [October 1 development note](DEVELOPMENT_UPDATE_2026-10-01_EN.md) records the scope and verification limits. The release date has not been confirmed.

## Verification priorities

- Confirm saving, unlocks and equipment persist after restarting
- Check input, focus, long translations and layouts at different resolutions
- Review animations and weapon contact across appearance, class and equipment combinations
- Check AI learning records, combat decisions, Ranked and Infinite Mode records
- Verify assets, localization and mode restrictions in packages and the demo

Priorities are adjusted using play checks and reproducible reports. Features and schedules will be announced once confirmed.
