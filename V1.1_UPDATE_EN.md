# TOOSIN : 투신 V1.1 Update

> Release date: August 23, 2026
> Highlights: Blood Contract balance, defensive Special Abilities, AI pattern learning, first-launch tutorial, localization, UI, and stability

V1.1 focuses on feedback from our players. The update especially addresses Blood Contracts with weak value or excessive penalties, defensive abilities that made offensive builds too safe, and AI learning that was difficult to notice during shorter fights.

## AI pattern learning: before and after

| Learning category | Before | After V1.1 |
|---|---|---|
| Learning updates | Long-term data was primarily calculated once when an enemy spawned | Long-term memory and current-combat observations are separated and continuously affect the same enemy's next decisions once enough evidence exists |
| Light and heavy attacks | Attack choices and combo hits could be mixed | Actual light/heavy starts and average combo length are measured separately |
| Approach, retreat, lateral movement | Movement frames could distort longer fights | Movement directions and average distance separately affect circling, backsteps, pursuit, and preferred range |
| Dodges and forward dodges | Attempts and successful dodges were not clearly separated | Only successful dodge starts are counted, with forward dodges tracked separately |
| Guard, parry, and hit outcomes | Defensive choices and results were not clearly separated | Dodge/guard choices and guard/parry/hit outcomes are analyzed independently |
| Attack timing and rhythm | Difficulty and random values had a larger influence | Real attack intervals affect reaction time, parry variation, attack pacing, and recovery punish decisions |
| Special abilities | Instant abilities could be missed | Successful activations are counted directly and affect response chances |
| Dodge-to-heavy attacks | No dedicated pattern existed | Heavy attacks within 0.8 seconds after a dodge are learned for recovery punishment and limited imitation |
| Connected pressure | Circling, dodging, and attacking were independent choices | With enough evidence, enemies can use `circle → forward dodge → settle → attack` as one connected tactic |

One or two actions never guarantee a counter. Learning affects future decisions after enough evidence is collected and does not cancel an attack or defense already in progress.

## Learning notifications and the TAB/Y panel

Broad `learning detected` messages now explain the observed habit and intended response. When at least four valid attack choices have been observed and heavy attacks account for 60% or more, the game can display:

> We remember the breath you take before putting your weight into a strike.
> The enemy will set their blade and wait to counter your heavy blow.

This means the enemy may adjust guard, dodge, and counterattack weights. It does not guarantee an automatic parry or counter against the next heavy attack.

| Displayed information | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Current confidence | How much usable evidence exists; not AI difficulty or win rate |
| Observed attacks | Ratio and sample count of actual light/heavy attack starts |
| Observed movement | Approach, retreat, and lateral movement ratios |
| Observed defense choice | Dodge and guard selection ratios |
| Observed hit outcomes | Guard, parry, and hit results |
| Applied learning values | Current influence on dodge, circling, and long-combo decisions |
| Planned response | Response being prepared or considered; may change with combat conditions |
| Last executed action | Most recent action actually performed using learned information |

- Hold `TAB` on keyboard or `Y` on gamepad to display the panel.
- It uses the locked-on enemy, or the nearest living enemy when no target is locked.
- The panel refreshes approximately every 0.25 seconds and hides immediately on release.
- Combat observations are analyzed approximately every 0.65 seconds for the next decision.
- It is automatically hidden in menus and pause screens.

Enemies can use Special Abilities starting from Stage 100 in Campaign Mode and Round 10 in Infinite Mode.

## Tutorial, regeneration, UI, and localization

- New save data begins with movement, camera, guard/parry, dodge, Special Ability, weapon switching, and AI-status tutorials before pattern learning.
- Opening Options during the tutorial no longer resets its current step.
- Automatic Health regeneration begins after two seconds without dealing or receiving damage and is shown as `N% of Max Health per second`.
- Added a shared font for Korean, English, Japanese, Simplified/Traditional Chinese, and Russian.
- Fixed long-text clipping and translation issues across events, Options, Training, Weapons/Combos, Stats, and AI logs.
- Trait nodes are cyan when unlocked, gold when currently unlockable, and dark gray when requirements are not met.
- Improved shared button states, gamepad focus, and window transitions.

## Steam and stability

- Prevented regular Steam stats from being submitted as leaderboard scores.
- Prevented zero-score submissions for modes the player has not entered.
- Stopped rejected identical scores from being submitted repeatedly.
- Serialized leaderboard lookup and upload requests.
- Safely clean up pending Steam requests during shutdown and map travel.

## Planned for V1.2

We plan to add new actions such as a shield bash performed while guarding, then expand enemy AI so it can observe, respond to, and learn from the wider behavior set.

Thank you to everyone who shared feedback.
