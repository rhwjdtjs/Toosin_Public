## Existing Perk Fixes and Description Improvements

The table below distinguishes gameplay changes from description corrections.

| Perk | Change Type | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |

| **Giant's Grip** | Effect adjustment | Defense now increases by **+3% at 1 stack, +6% at 2 stacks, and +6% at 3 stacks**. At 3 stacks, the existing **20% chance to gain Super Armor for 1 second when hit** also applies. |

| **Thunderbolt** | Description correction | Clarified that the perk grants **+5 percentage points of armor penetration per stack** and, **starting at 1 stack, a 15% chance to deal 1.2x damage on a hit**. |

Combo Master's combo stacks carry over after a normal combo ends. Existing reset conditions, such as taking a hit or explicitly canceling, still apply.

Entries marked **Description correction** clarify existing effects; they do not increase or decrease their values. Percentage points are added to the existing rate.

## Perk and Trait Stability Improvements

- Fixed removed perk bonuses remaining in your stats immediately after resetting perks.
- Fixed attack-speed buffs or next-attack bonuses remaining after Shield Bash or Combo Master was removed.
- Fixed duplicate trait records in save data applying the same stat bonuses more than once.
- New perks retain their stack limits when saved, loaded, or reapplied, without repeatedly adding the same effects.
- Existing trait SP refunds are accounted for first. Additional refunds from new perks are limited to the remaining amount of SP actually spent on that attack.
- Repeated contact from the same attack, burn damage, and additional trait damage no longer repeatedly trigger the new weapon-hit effects.

## Combining Perks with Existing Traits and Contracts

- **Guard-breaking traits + Collapse Pursuit:** Break an enemy's guard with an existing trait, then follow up against that enemy. The hit that causes the guard break does not also receive the follow-up reward.
- **Lifesteal + Blood Refinement:** Improve actual healing based on your existing lifesteal and contract modifiers. Blood Refinement does not grant lifesteal on its own. Its bonus barrier at 3 stacks is added without reducing the HP you restore.
- **Status effects + Ember Wedge or Frost Shatter:** Strengthen attacks against burning, slowed, or frozen enemies. Frost Shatter's additional bonus at 3 stacks requires an actual freeze.
- **Blood contracts + Oath Seal:** The number of distinct contracts you hold increases poise damage and the barrier granted by your first parry of the round.

**Guard break, guard SP damage, and poise damage are separate conditions and effects.** Collapse Pursuit strengthens attacks after a guard break; it does not automatically break guards.

Weighted Strike enters the normal reward pool once charged attacks are unlocked. Oath Seal enters the pool when you hold at least 1 contract. Temporary buffs and target-specific records reset between rounds, while owned perk stacks are retained.
