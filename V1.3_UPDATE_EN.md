# Update 1.3 — 100 Advanced Traits, Infinite Mode Results and Reward Guides, and Clearer Combat

This document records changes for Version 1.3. The [October 1 development note](DEVELOPMENT_UPDATE_2026-10-01_EN.md) describes the preparation and verification status of 1.4.

[Version 1.2](V1.2_UPDATE_EN.md) · [Version 1.3](V1.3_UPDATE_EN.md) · [Version 1.3.1](V1.3.1_UPDATE_EN.md) · [한국어](V1.3_UPDATE_KR.md)

Hello everyone,
We have prepared Update 1.3 around your feedback—from more uses for spare Trait Points to Infinite Mode results, clearer rank bonus explanations, the atmosphere of the arena, and combat feedback.

In particular, we have clarified confusing explanations such as “Why am I receiving a third-place bonus when I am fourth in Ranked?” You can now see which mode's bonus is actually being applied to your stats.

## Key Changes: Before → After

This summary covers additions since the previous release, along with improvements made in response to follow-up feedback during 1.3 development.

## 1. 100 Advanced Traits — Unlock Them and Keep Their Effects

T6–T10 have been added to all five categories: Strength, Agility, Vitality, Spirit, and Technique. Each tier in each category contains four traits, for a total of 100. Existing T1–T5 traits remain unchanged.

- **Cost per trait: T6 20 / T7 25 / T8 30 / T9 35 / T10 40 TP**. Unlocking all of them costs **3,000 TP**.
- **Unlocked traits remain active without a separate equip step.** There are no presets, equip/unequip steps, trait capacity limits, or limits on the number of active T10 traits.
- Prerequisite traits are still required. Remaining active means the trait is owned and enabled; conditional effects still require their hit, damage taken, guard, dodge, or other activation conditions to be met.
- The heavy-attack hyper armour that duplicated an existing T5 trait has been replaced with a heavy-attack retaliation effect against the enemy that hurt you. A duplicate revival effect has also been replaced with recovery through weapon hits after building up the required conditions.
- The names and effects of every newly added trait are listed by category and tier at the end of this announcement.

## 2. New Infinite Mode Rank Bonuses and Their Application

**Infinite Mode leaderboard stat bonuses have been added. Existing Ranked rewards remain available.** Normal combat uses **one bonus package: the best rank 1–9 bonus among the latest platform-verified ranks for each mode in this session**—Stage, Ranked Season 2, and Infinite Mode. The three bonuses do not stack. An earlier best rank is not retained after a confirmed drop in rank, and unverified historical records do not apply.

For example, if you are fourth in Ranked Season 2 and third in Infinite Mode, the Infinite Mode third-place bonus is the one applied. The Ranked screen now identifies its source as **“Active shared bonus · Infinite, rank 3”**, while the Ranked fourth-place values are shown separately for comparison.

- Press **“Rank bonus guide”** in Infinite Mode to expand the active-bonus summary and full reward table. The body can be scrolled, while the Start and Close buttons remain available.
- You can check the applied values in the HP, stamina, attack, and defence calculations on the player stat screen, under the **(rating)** entries. Stats and their displayed values update together when the rank changes; reopening the screen does not duplicate the bonus.
- Fixed a calculation path where fixed rank bonuses could be reduced when included in proportional adjustments such as contracts. Rank bonuses themselves do not award TP or PP, and they do not apply in training that excludes progression bonuses.
- Strengthened account identification so another player with the same nickname cannot overwrite your verified Steam rank.

## 3. Infinite Mode Defeat Results — How Far Did This Attempt Go?

After a defeat in Infinite Mode, returning to the main menu now shows the results of that attempt.

- **The stage reached** and **the stage cleared** are shown separately. For example, a defeat at stage 12 is displayed as “Reached 12 / Cleared 11.”
- Your lifetime best is not presented as the result of the current attempt. Scheduling the next stage does not count as reaching it before it actually starts.
- The result is shown only once. Leaving manually or an enemy-spawn failure does not produce a defeat result.
- The entry description now clarifies the distinction: Infinite Mode does not grant normal stage-clear rewards, but verified leaderboard rank bonuses still apply under the shared rules.
- Long translations can be scrolled within the body, while the confirmation button stays fixed. Keyboard and gamepad scrolling, confirmation, and closing are supported.

## 4. Ranked Season 2 — Distinguishing Points, Contribution, and Faction Rewards

Personal rank is still determined by cumulative Season 2 points. Territory influence is not a simple sum of those points: it represents **contribution from victories for that territory and faction over the last 14 days**.

- **Confirmed contribution over the last 14 days** appears as a small supplementary line below each player's original leaderboard score. The original score and ranking criteria are unchanged.
- If an older version has no personal daily history, the display reads “no history.” If only part of the period is available, it reads “collecting.” Unknown past records are not arbitrarily replaced with zero or with the season score.
- Strengthened recovery by retaining contributions awaiting submission and comparing them with the latest server values when retrying.
- When your faction leads in a territory, members receive up to +6% maximum HP/stamina and +3% attack/defence during Ranked matches in that territory. **Joining a faction does not grant a permanent bonus across every mode.**
- The leading faction faces stronger enemy resistance and greater risk. The trailing faction faces less pressure, while points, win streaks, combat adaptation, and terrain also affect risk. Risk and territorial advantage are also reflected in victory points.
- The leaderboard reward guide and territory selection screen explain rank rewards, faction boost conditions, and the values expected for the match.

## 5. Late-Game Enemy Traits and Combat Visuals

Added **42 status indicators** for Fractures, weak points, echoes, embers, barriers, momentum and prepared attacks. Player and current-target states are shown separately, including relevant stacks, values and remaining time.

- Enemies in regular stages, ambushes, and Infinite Mode also receive usable T6–T10 paths according to progression and dynamic difficulty adjustment (DDA). They are not assigned automatically before actual stage 60.
- Without a difficulty adjustment, the maximum tier expands to T6 at 60, T7 at 100, T8 at 160, T9 at 240, and T10 at 320. DDA can adjust the progression value used for selection by up to ±40. Automatic assignment is limited to 20 advanced traits per enemy.
- Ranked opponents use their recorded trait builds. This does not limit the player's trait count to 20 or overwrite Ranked records with automatically assigned builds.
- Traits have been connected to visuals suited to their roles, including captured-pose afterimages, fractures, marks, wards, recovery, cleansing, and ground waves. Their position, direction, and range follow the actual activation.
- Enemies can use shared combat effects when they own the Trait and meet its actual conditions. Ordinary dodges do not count as perfect dodges; supported abilities, valid targets and allies, and successful actions remain necessary.
- Strengthened missing connections for cross-dodge effects, Ranked trait activation before the first action, and AI handling of attack types blocked by analysis effects.

We have also reduced short start-stop movements and enemies moving back and forth around the same position. Observed player patterns affect guarding, dodging, reaction time, and preferred distance, while formations follow rules based on roles and attack timing.

## 6. Impact Feedback, Blood Effects, and Target Visibility

- Removed the fixed crescent blade effect that appeared detached from the sword and replaced it with a brief, transparent air trail that follows the actual blade. Elemental auras and special-ability effects remain.
- Added short camera responses for hits, heavy strikes, critical hits, incoming damage, guards, parries, and guard breaks. Overlapping responses are limited, and camera-shake settings are respected.
- Increased blood-effect output to an average of 1.5× the previous adjustment. Individual effect size, colour, and the simultaneous-effect cap in busy fights are unchanged, so the actual number emitted may be reduced when the limit is reached.
- Added a short contact-dust effect to confirmed heavy and critical hits.
- Ranked group fights prioritize the nearest valid enemy. In both Ranked and Infinite Mode, the current target receives a white outline only while at least two enemies are alive. It turns off when one enemy remains and does not fill the entire body with white.

## 7. A Weathered Roman-Style Arena and Graphics Cost Adjustments

We have refined the sand, soil, stone, red cloth and flags, and bronze decorations in the main arena and tutorial map, strengthening their weathering and wear. Cloud materials and atmosphere settings have also been adjusted.

Large overhead structural shadows are less likely to obscure the centre of combat, while the overly bright ground has been adjusted towards deeper earth tones and warmer colours. Character and architectural shadows have not all been removed.

Arena debris that looked like large white masses has been revised into small, low grey and beige fragments. Added decorations do not affect collision or pathfinding. Crowd shadows and reflections, effect and decoration draw distances, and simultaneous-use limits have also been adjusted. These changes do not promise a specific FPS improvement on any particular hardware.

## 8. First Combat Practice and UI/Input Improvements

- Added a training bot without a health bar to the first tutorial. Light attacks are taught through actual hits from the 1→2→3 combo, while heavy attacks require one actual hit.
- Fixed paths where the bot failed to approach or attacked too late during guard and parry practice. Holding guard in advance no longer stalls the guide.
- Training briefly pauses just before guard, parry, and perfect-dodge timing to show the currently assigned input, then progresses according to actual success. Kicks also require an actual hit, and any remaining paused state is cleared when ending or skipping the guide.
- Fixed narrow wrapping and clipping in trait descriptions, settlement text, and perk names, keeping the main buttons inside their windows.
- Main-menu buttons remain usable while achievement notifications are displayed. Result, merchant, and contract windows that require a choice retain their confirmation and closing steps.
- Improved the issue where A confirmation could be cancelled when the gamepad cursor overlapped the selected button, along with weapon-selection navigation, movement within popups, and focus restoration after closing.
- Strengthened conflict detection, cancellation, and actual application of keyboard, mouse, and gamepad bindings. Removed unnecessary weapon-card text and made special-ability names and button labels white.
- Removed the three unnecessary headings that remained at the lower right of the stat screen in some languages.

## 9. Perk Reward Explanations and Six Languages

- **Increased chance of higher-tier stage rewards:** Increases the selection weights of Rare, Legendary, and Special rewards in actual stage rewards during the current run, stacking up to three times. It does not grant another new perk.
- **Guarantee a Rare-or-better reward at the next stage reward draw:** Guarantees an obtainable Rare-or-better reward for the next actual reward draw. If no eligible reward exists, the guarantee is not consumed.
- **Trait Points +1:** Used to unlock traits, including the new advanced traits. Once all existing and advanced traits for the current class are confirmed unlocked, this repeat reward card is no longer offered.
- Collecting every reward does not create an endless supply of new rewards, nor does unlocking every trait create an unlimited additional use for points.
- Trait, perk, rank reward, and Infinite Mode result explanations are available in Korean, English, Japanese, Russian, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese.
- On first launch, the game uses the operating system's display language if it is supported, or English otherwise. An existing saved supported-language setting is preserved.

We have also strengthened related paths for point conversion and saving, duplicate rewards, purchases and contract selections, large experience/level/point calculations, and separation of training and mock-combat progression.

If a problem occurs again, please include the mode, stage, weapon, input device, and steps that led to it in your report. We will continue to work from your feedback.

## The complete T6–T10 advanced trait list

Each of the five categories—Strength, Agility, Vitality, Spirit and Technique—gains four progression paths, with five traits per path from T6 to T10. That is **5 categories × 4 paths × 5 tiers = 100 traits**.

- **Unlocked traits remain owned and active without being equipped.** There are no presets, equipment-capacity limits or active-T10 limits. Each trait still requires its actual attack, dodge, parry, Health, mark or other activation condition.
- **Cost per trait: T6 20 / T7 25 / T8 30 / T9 35 / T10 40 TP.** Each category costs 600 TP; all 100 cost **3,000 TP**. Existing T1–T5 unlock costs are not included in that total.
- T6 requires an existing T5 trait in the same category. T7–T10 require the preceding trait in the same path. Prior TP spent on advanced traits in that category must also reach 0 / 20 / 100 / 210 / 350 respectively. These spending thresholds are not extra fees.
- The descriptions below focus on activation conditions. Damage, healing and resource refunds remain subject to mode-specific scaling, cooldowns and caps. Trait effects do not recursively trigger unlimited additional effects.
- Input-based traits use your currently mapped dodge or guard control; they do not add new dedicated hotkeys.

## Strength — 20 traits

### T6 — 20 TP per trait

- **Fracture Brand** — A fully charged heavy attack leaves a Fracture on the enemy.
- **Payback** — Lose at least 15% of maximum Health to one unguarded hit, then heavy-hit that attacker within 6 seconds for additional poise damage. Cooldown: 8 seconds.
- **Wide Arc** — Hitting at least 2 different enemies with one attack restores stamina.
- **Mark Prey** — Keep an elite or boss targeted to reveal its poise and incoming heavy attacks.

### T7 — 25 TP per trait

- **Stance Break** — Land a heavy hit on an enemy with 3 Fractures to consume them and deal additional poise damage.
- **Life Collateral** — When stamina is insufficient, pay 3% of maximum Health to use an empowered heavy attack. Health is spent only when the attack actually starts.
- **Formation Break** — A heavy attack that hits at least 3 enemies scatters their formation outward.
- **Opening Pressure** — Landing a light, heavy, and counter hit creates a weak point on the target.

### T8 — 30 TP per trait

- **Shock Spread** — Breaking a Fracture spreads its shock to up to 2 nearby enemies.
- **Battle Pulse** — Alternating light and heavy hits charges an empowered heavy attack.
- **Chain Collision** — A pushed enemy colliding with another deals poise damage to both.
- **Punishing Leap** — After a perfect dodge or parry, your next heavy attack closes in on the target, only when collision checks allow a safe move.

### T9 — 35 TP per trait

- **Shard Recovery** — Break a guard with a heavy attack to recover half the stamina actually spent on that heavy attack and gain a brief recovery-cancel opportunity.
- **Blood Reckoning** — An empowered heavy hit restores the Health spent and part of the heavy damage you banked.
- **Encirclement Reversal** — When at least 3 enemies are within 6 m, prepare an all-direction attack for your next heavy strike.
- **Giant's Recoil** — Perfectly countering a boss heavy attack deals percentage poise damage.

### T10 — 40 TP per trait

- **Seismic Rupture** — Breaking a guard creates a non-chaining ground shockwave within 6 m.
- **Limit Break** — At 30% maximum Health or below, alternating light and heavy hits restores stamina.
- **Linebreaker** — After hitting 4 different enemies, your next heavy attack launches a linear shockwave.
- **Execution Angle** — Hit a target at 8% maximum Health or below: execute a normal enemy, or prepare a poise weakness for the next heavy attack against an elite or boss.

## Agility — 20 traits

### T6 — 20 TP per trait

- **Afterimage Decoy** — A successful perfect dodge leaves a brief decoy at that location to draw enemy attacks, using your actual character pose and equipped weapon/shield appearance.
- **Acceleration Axis** — Keep moving to build up to 5 Momentum stacks. Momentum decays when you stop.
- **Blindside** — Attacking an enemy from behind leaves a Shadow Mark.
- **Reserve Breath** — Maintain full stamina while avoiding hits to store 1 free dodge.

### T7 — 25 TP per trait

- **Reverse Step** — While the afterimage remains valid after a perfect dodge, your first melee follow-up slips toward the target's flank or rear. It does not force you through blocked space.
- **Preserved Direction** — Dodging or making a sharp turn no longer immediately clears Momentum. It still decays when you stop.
- **Cross Dodge** — Complete a dodge that actually crosses the enemy's left/right sides to gain Direction tokens for a stronger flank or rear follow-up. Interrupted dodges and movement in place do not qualify.
- **Short Dodge** — Dodges chained within 1 second travel less distance and cost less.

### T8 — 30 TP per trait

- **Steal Time** — Normal enemies that attack the afterimage are briefly slowed.
- **Momentum Slash** — At maximum Momentum, prepare a Momentum Slash for your next attack. A confirmed hit and a safe path allow the rush through the target.
- **Triangular Pressure** — Completing front, flank, and rear attacks triggers a poise burst.
- **Exhausted Footwork** — Perform an emergency free dodge even when stamina is insufficient. Emergency-dodge cooldown: 20 seconds.

### T9 — 35 TP per trait

- **Trace Recovery** — Countering an enemy that attacked the afterimage refunds dodge stamina.
- **Rush Relay** — A Momentum Slash kill automatically targets the nearest next enemy.
- **Isolated Hunt** — Killing a marked enemy isolated from its allies restores dodge resources.
- **Breath Recovery** — For 2.5 seconds after a perfect dodge, stamina can recover even while attacking. This is a recovery window, not just an instant refund.

### T10 — 40 TP per trait

- **Ghost Shift** — Press dodge again within the afterimage's 3-second lifetime to return to it once. The return is rejected if collision or an attack in progress at the decoy makes it unsafe.
- **Trail Burst** — Using a special ability produces one reduced-power replay along the recorded areas of your recent movement path. Your character does not run the route again.
- **Shadowless Flurry** — With 3 recent direct hits recorded, strike a Shadow-Marked enemy to replay part of their combined damage once and display the recorded body, weapon and shield poses in sequence.
- **Four-Way Step** — You may change direction once during a perfect dodge.

## Vitality — 20 traits

### T6 — 20 TP per trait

- **Overflowing Life** — Converts excess healing into a capped barrier.
- **Emergency Suture** — At 30% maximum Health or below, complete the suture's no-hit waiting period to receive delayed healing.
- **Rooted** — Ignore one hit reaction while attacking or guarding, with a 2-second cooldown after activation. This is not continuous super armor throughout a heavy attack.
- **Rescue Signal** — Link to a low-Health ally and share part of heavy damage. Solo: becomes a personal barrier.

### T7 — 25 TP per trait

- **Impact Absorption** — While the barrier holds, it prevents the first hit reaction.
- **Wound Target** — Attacking the enemy that hurt you reduces the suture delay.
- **Breakfall** — After being knocked down by a shove, press dodge to spend stamina and get up faster.
- **Shared Life** — Spread shared damage over 4 seconds. This shared damage itself cannot reduce your Health below 1.

### T8 — 30 TP per trait

- **Rupture Barrier** — When the barrier breaks, it deals poise impact to nearby enemies.
- **Coagulation** — Damage over time pauses suture healing instead of canceling it.
- **Status Expulsion** — With Burn, Chill or Shock active, press dodge to spend 18 stamina and remove one status. A successful cleanse consumes that input instead of also performing a dodge.
- **Cover Rotation** — When the linked ally successfully guards or perfect-dodges, both gain a barrier.

### T9 — 35 TP per trait

- **Reclaiming Armor** — When the remaining barrier expires, part of it becomes stamina.
- **Gray Life** — Stores canceled suture healing and restores it when the Wound Target is killed.
- **Adaptive Resistance** — Briefly adapts to the type of stagger, push, or knockdown suffered.
- **Rescue Counter** — Countering an enemy that attacked an ally heals that ally.

### T10 — 40 TP per trait

- **Regenerative Veil** — Avoid damage long enough to create a barrier. Large single hits are capped periodically.
- **Second Pulse** — After a suture completes, direct hits heal 25% of damage for 6 seconds, up to 12% of maximum Health in total. This is not a resurrection effect.
- **Walking Fortress** — Resisting control briefly hardens you but reduces mobility.
- **Final Bond** — Once per round, prevents lethal damage to the linked ally.

## Spirit — 20 traits

### T6 — 20 TP per trait

- **Parry Echo** — A successful parry stores 1 Echo.
- **Ember Recovery** — Kills while your special ability is on cooldown grant Embers.
- **Purifying Breath** — Stay unharmed at full stamina to remove one debuff.
- **Rallying Rhythm** — A perfect dodge or parry restores stamina to nearby allies.

### T7 — 25 TP per trait

- **Echo Cast** — Spend a stored Echo to replay your next special ability's supported effect once at reduced power. A supported ability and valid target/location are required.
- **Stored Cast** — The longer you leave a ready special ability unused, the greater the poise wave on its next cast. Time spent on cooldown does not count.
- **Inversion** — Briefly return a cleansed debuff to its source and store a sigil that blocks one debuff of the same type. Against bosses, the reflected status becomes poise damage instead.
- **Answering Call** — Countering an enemy that attacked an ally grants that ally a barrier.

### T8 — 30 TP per trait

- **Resonant Guard** — When an Echo is actually spent on Echo Cast, nearby allies gain a barrier.
- **Split Circuit** — Hit at least 2 different enemies with one special ability to receive one cooldown refund. If no cooldown can be refunded, restore stamina instead.
- **Untainted Sigil** — Maintain full stamina without taking hits while free of debuffs to store a sigil that blocks the next debuff.
- **Shared Mark** — Attacking the same enemy as an ally creates a weak point shared by the party.

### T9 — 35 TP per trait

- **Counterfrequency** — During a brief grace period after the parry window expires, spend an Echo to convert a late hit into a normal guard. Forced guard-break attacks are excluded.
- **Lingering Field** — Leave a sigil for 3 seconds at the location where the special ability was used. It restores stamina to you and nearby allies within its area.
- **Composure Restored** — Stamina recovery begins immediately after removing a debuff.
- **Saving Wave** — Your next parry heals nearby allies in critical condition.

### T10 — 40 TP per trait

- **Perfect Echo** — After 2 consecutive parries, replay the supported effect of your next special ability once from the opposite side of the target, with an actual-pose afterimage at the replay location.
- **Dual Rite** — Collect 5 Embers to store one reduced-power recast of a supported special ability. A successful replay on the next cast consumes the Embers and stored recast.
- **Untainted Soul** — When the sigil triggers, briefly become immune to debuffs and weaken the attacker.
- **Spirit of the Field** — When an ally falls, surviving allies who own this trait are briefly empowered. With no allies in solo combat, it instead grants you a crisis barrier and temporary empowerment.

## Technique — 20 traits

### T6 — 20 TP per trait

- **Threefold Form** — Records the order of 3 confirmed light or heavy hits as a Form.
- **Observation Point** — Observing one enemy reveals weak-point timing during its attacks.
- **Recoil Cancel** — When an attack is blocked, briefly cancel its recovery into a different attack.
- **Threat Log** — Records enemy attack types: quick, heavy, and ranged.

### T7 — 25 TP per trait

- **Form Effect** — Completed Forms grant either a free dodge, poise damage, or stamina.
- **Critical Line** — A hit during weak-point timing interrupts the attack or deals poise damage.
- **Read the Guard** — Following a blocked attack with a different type deals more guard damage.
- **Response Card** — A successful response suited to the attack type stores a Response token.

### T8 — 30 TP per trait

- **Continuation** — Successful guards, parries or perfect dodges briefly preserve the Form in progress. Merely pressing dodge does not preserve it.
- **Error Correction** — After enough non-critical weak-point hits, the next weak-point hit is critical.
- **Feint** — Press guard during an attack to perform a harmless feint that provokes a defensive response from a normal enemy. It does not force this reaction on bosses.
- **Tailored Response** — A follow-up hit spends a stored token: quick attacks grant slow, heavy attacks grant poise damage, and ranged attacks grant both stamina recovery and protection from the next hit reaction. Against bosses, the slow becomes poise damage.

### T9 — 35 TP per trait

- **Accent** — Precise recovery input strengthens the next Form's effect.
- **Excess Precision** — Converts part of critical chance above the cap into armor penetration.
- **Create an Opening** — Creates a brief weak point on enemies that react to the feint.
- **Tactical Sharing** — Shares analyzed attack data and weak-point timing with allies.

### T10 — 40 TP per trait

- **Endless Variation** — Complete different Forms in succession to reduce special-ability cooldown and gain a 2-second attack-recovery cancel window.
- **Single-Point Pierce** — After 3 weak-point hits, your next heavy attack pierces defense.
- **Formless** — Complete a light hit → successful guard → heavy hit → dodge counter in order to freely cancel attack recovery for 3 seconds.
- **Solved Pattern** — Briefly seals one well-analyzed attack type of a normal enemy. Against bosses, weakens poise instead.

### Afterimages, visual effects and enemy AI

- Afterimage Decoy, Shadowless Flurry and Perfect Echo use actual character poses and equipment. **This does not add 100 new attack montages**, and the visual afterimages do not apply damage a second time.
- Fractures, weak points, barriers, sutures, cleansing, parries and ground waves use role-specific visual cues. Preservation and cost-modifier traits share the relevant activation cue; not every node creates a separate explosion.
- Effect settings and visual budgets may omit a cue. Omitting its visual does not disable the unlocked trait's combat effect.
- Enemies can use shared combat effects when they own the Trait and perform the required action. They do not automatically own all 100 Traits or succeed in every situation. Perfect-dodge effects require an actual successful perfect dodge; charged attacks, supported abilities, valid allies and targets remain subject to their individual requirements.
